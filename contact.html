FAQ

Good to know.

Can't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll get back to you during office hours.

Who do you supply?

Distributors, supermarkets, grocery stores, bakeries and other retailers. FineBites is a wholesale supplier, so we work with businesses rather than individual shoppers.

Which countries do you export to?

Most of our exports go to Africa, including Chad, Kenya and many other markets across the continent. We also supply across the UAE and the wider GCC. If your country isn't listed, message us and we'll let you know how we can ship to you.

How quickly will I hear back?

We reply to every inquiry within 24 hours, Sunday to Thursday. WhatsApp is usually the fastest way to reach us.

What should I include in my inquiry?

The products or categories you're interested in, the approximate quantity (units or cartons), your company name and your delivery location. That lets us send accurate pricing straight away.
